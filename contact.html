Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to make a table reservation in advance?

Walk-in guests are always warmly welcomed! However, for Friday to Sunday dinner services or parties larger than 4 guests, we strongly recommend reserving ahead via our form or WhatsApp to avoid waiting times.

Do you offer pure vegetarian and Jain culinary options?

Yes, absolutely! We maintain separate vegetarian preparation cookware and offer customized Jain food preparations (no onion, no garlic) for paneer gravies, dals, and biryanis upon request.

Is valet parking available at the restaurant?

Yes, complimentary valet parking is provided right outside our main entrance on Lane 7, Koregaon Park for all dining guests.

How does direct WhatsApp ordering work?

Simply click the "Order on WhatsApp" button on any dish or use the cart on our Menu page. It creates a ready-to-send text message with your dishes and subtotal. Our team confirms your delivery address and shares UPI payment options!
