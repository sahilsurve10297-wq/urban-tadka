Our Culinary Heritage

A TASTE WORTH
COMING BACK FOR

Founded in the vibrant heart of Pune, The Urban Tadka was born from a singular passion: to elevate authentic Indian soul food into a world-class dining celebration without compromising on roots.

In Indian culinary lore, the "Tadka" (tempering) is the defining soul of every dish — that precise magical moment when whole mustard seeds, cumin, roasted garlic, and fiery Kashmiri chilies sizzle in pure desi ghee, unlocking an explosion of fragrance.

From our succulent char-grilled kebabs to velvety handi curries and aromatic dum biryanis, every single preparation is balanced to tantalize your palate.
